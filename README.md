# Biochemistry-carbohydrate-enzymology-paper

This second paper in the biochemistry trilogy uses the fatty-acid framework established in the previous paper to explore advanced enzymology used in carbohydrate biochemistry. 

This paper emphasizes: 
- Carbohydrate biochemistry and its reaction mechanisms;
- Glycation biochemistry;
- Transition-state biochemistry specific to carbohydrate and glycation frameworks.
 
This paper concludes with an introduction to mechanistic nucleic-acid biochemistry and a mathematical proof of the Bloomfield-Alberty theorem, bridging this paper to the third and final paper of the series.
